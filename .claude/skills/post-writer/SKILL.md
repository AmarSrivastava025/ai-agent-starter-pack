# Post Writer

**The problem it solves:** "I never know what to post, and what I write does not sound like me."

## Before you start
1. Follow the rules in `CLAUDE.md`. Read `profile/profile.md` and `profile/voice-samples.md`.
2. If `profile/voice-samples.md` is still empty, say: "I write best when I can see how you really write. Please paste 2 or 3 things you have written, into profile/voice-samples.md, or paste them here." If they paste them here, use them for this run and offer to save them in that file.
3. Say in one line what you are about to do: "I will ask you about one real experience, then write three short versions of a post. You pick one."

## What to ask for
Ask ONE question at a time and wait.
1. Tell me about one thing that really happened at work or with someone you helped. In 2 or 3 sentences: what was the situation, what did you do, what did you learn?
2. Which platform: LinkedIn, Instagram or Facebook?
3. Is there anything private in this? Please do not name a client or a colleague unless they have said yes, and nothing confidential from your employer.

If the person has no experience in mind, ask: "What question do friends or colleagues ask you most often?" and use that as the starting point. Do not make up an experience.

## The result
Write THREE versions. Each one:
- 5 to 8 short lines.
- Starts with the situation, not with "I".
- Ends with one question for the reader.
- Uses plain words and the person's own voice. No exclamation marks, no emoji unless their samples use them, no sales pitch, no promise of income or results.
- Uses only what the person told you. If you need a detail they did not give, leave it out or ask.

After the three versions, ask: "Which one is closest to you? Tell me one thing to change and I will adjust it."

## Save and finish
- Save the versions and the final choice in `outputs/post-writer/<today's date>-<platform>.md`.
- Tell the person: "Nothing has been posted. Copy it and post it yourself."
- Finish with the file name in one line, and: "Come back with another experience any time. One real story a week is enough."

## Do not
- Do not invent details, names, numbers or results.
- Do not write anything the person has not lived.
