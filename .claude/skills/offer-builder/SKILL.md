# Offer Builder

**The problem it solves:** "I know who I help, but I have nothing concrete to put in front of them."

## Before you start
1. Follow the rules in `CLAUDE.md`. Read `profile/profile.md`.
2. The "My niche" and "My ideal customer" parts should be filled in. If they are empty, say which one is missing and suggest /niche-finder or /ideal-customer-interviewer first. If the person wants to go on anyway, ask them to tell you the niche and the customer in two lines, and use only that.
3. Say in one line what you are about to do: "I will ask you what you plan to do with the person, then write your one-page offer."

## The interview
Ask ONE question at a time and wait. Skip what the profile already answers.
1. If you have had a real conversation with someone like your customer, what did they say? Tell me in their words. (If not yet, say so. That is fine.)
2. What do you plan to do with the person? Tell me in 3 to 5 simple steps, in your own words.
3. How long does it take, in time or number of sessions?
4. What does the person need to bring or prepare?
5. What is NOT included? Name at least one thing.
6. How many hours a week can you really give to this, next to your job?

## The result
Write a one-page offer with exactly these headings, using only what the person told you:
1. Who it is for
2. The problem in their words
3. What we will do together (3 to 5 plain steps)
4. How long it takes
5. What they need to bring
6. What is NOT included
7. How we start
8. Price: write "[my price]". Never suggest a price or a range.

Where something is missing, write a short question in [square brackets] and say what the person should find out. If they have not had a real conversation yet, write "[to check after my first conversation]" where the customer's own words would go. Do not leave the offer half finished, and do not make anything up.

Then add: **3 worries a careful person may have about this offer**, each with a calm, honest answer of 2 sentences. Where you are not sure of an answer, write "I need to decide this". No pressure and no guarantees.

## Save and finish
- Save in `outputs/offer-builder/<today's date>-run.md`.
- Tell the person: "Every [square bracket] is something only you can fill in. Fill them in, then tell me, and I will tidy the final version."
- Ask: "May I save your offer in your profile?" Only after a yes, fill in the "My offer" part of `profile/profile.md`.
- Finish with the file name in one line, and: "Next: run /profile-optimizer."

## Do not
- Do not invent steps, results, timelines or clients. Do not promise income, clients or a result. Do not name a price.
