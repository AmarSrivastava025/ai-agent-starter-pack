# Niche Finder

**The problem it solves:** "I have years of experience, but I do not know what to sell or who I would help."

## Before you start
1. Follow the rules in `CLAUDE.md`. Read `profile/profile.md`.
2. Say in one line what you are about to do: "I will ask you a few short questions about your work, then show you five possible niches. You choose one."
3. If the "Who I am" part of the profile is already filled in, do not ask those questions again. Say what you already know in two lines, and ask only what is missing.

## The interview
Ask ONE question at a time and wait for the answer. Ask only what is missing, in this order:
1. What is your first name?
2. What is your job today, and how many years have you done it?
3. What do colleagues, friends or family come to you for help with?
4. Tell me about one thing at work you are proud of. What really happened?
5. What do you do easily that other people find hard?
6. Think of one real person you have helped. What was their problem? (If there is nobody yet, skip this.)
7. How many hours a week can you honestly give?
8. Is there anything you do not want to do? For example cold calling, travel, working with children.

Short answers are fine. If they say "I do not know", accept it and go on to the next question.

## The result
Use only what they told you. Write:

**A. Your 5 strongest skills.** Each one with their own real example, in their words.

**B. Five niches to consider, best first.** A niche is one kind of person with one problem. For each one:
- Who needs it (one kind of person)
- Their problem (one line, in plain words)
- Why it fits you (from their answers)
- What could get in the way (include their hours and the things they do not want to do)
- One small test this week (a real person to talk to, nothing to sell)

**C. Your first pick.** Say which one you would test first and why, in two lines. Then say clearly: "This is only a suggestion. You decide." Ask: "Which one do you choose?" and wait.

**D. After they choose.** Write three versions of one line: "I help [one kind of person] [get the result they want] without [the worry that holds them back]." At most 20 words each, in everyday words. Ask which one sounds most like them.

## Save and finish
- Save the skills, the five niches, their choice and the line they picked in `outputs/niche-finder/<today's date>-run.md`.
- Ask: "May I save your skills, your niche and your line in your profile?" Only after a yes, fill in the matching parts of `profile/profile.md`.
- Finish with the file name in one line, and: "Next: run /ideal-customer-interviewer."

## Do not
- Do not choose the niche for them.
- Do not invent skills, customers, results or numbers. Do not mention money.
