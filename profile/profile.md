# My profile

Fill this in once. Every agent reads it. You can edit it any time. Short answers are fine.
Replace everything in [square brackets]. If you do not know a part yet, leave it empty: the agents will help you find it.

## Who I am
- My name: [first name]
- My job today and years of experience: [for example: finance manager, 12 years in banking]
- What people come to me for help with: [a few lines]
- One thing at work I am proud of: [2 or 3 sentences about something that really happened]
- Things I do easily that others find hard: [a few lines]

## My time
- Hours a week I can give: [a number]
- Things I do not want to do: [for example: cold calling, travel, working with children]

## My niche (the Niche Finder fills this)
- The kind of person I help: [one kind of person]
- The problem I help them with: [one problem, in the words they would use]
- My one-line statement: [I help ... without ...]

## My ideal customer (the Ideal Customer Interviewer fills this)
- Who they are: [job, situation, age if it matters]
- What keeps them stuck: [in their words]
- What they have already tried: [a few lines]
- Where they spend time online: [for example: LinkedIn, WhatsApp groups]

## My offer (the Offer Builder fills this)
- Name of my offer: [short name]
- What I do with them, step by step: [3 to 5 plain steps]
- How long it takes, what they bring, what is not included: [a few lines]

## My profiles online (the Profile Optimizer reads this)
- Where I show up: [LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook ...]
- Links: [paste your profile links]

## My voice (the Post Writer reads this)
- Words I like to use: [a few]
- Words I never use: [a few]
- Paste 3 to 5 things you have really written (posts, messages) in `voice-samples.md`.
