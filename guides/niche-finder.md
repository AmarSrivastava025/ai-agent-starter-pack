# Niche Finder

**The problem it solves**
"I have years of experience, but I do not know what to sell or who I would help."

**Who it is for**
Anyone who has skills and experience but cannot yet say who they help.

**Before you start**
- Your profile is in the folder: `profile/profile.md`. You can leave it mostly empty: this agent fills in your niche.

**How to run it**
Open Claude Code in this folder and type: `/niche-finder`
It asks you a few short questions, one at a time. Answer in your own words. Short answers are fine.

**What you get**
Your 5 strongest skills, five niches to consider (one kind of person with one problem each), a first pick as a suggestion, and one line you can say about who you help. Saved in `outputs/niche-finder/`.

**What it will not do**
It will not invent facts about you, promise money or clients, or post anything for you. You stay in charge of every decision.
