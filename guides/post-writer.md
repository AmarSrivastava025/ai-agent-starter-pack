# Post Writer

**The problem it solves**
"I never know what to post, and what I write does not sound like me."

**Who it is for**
Anyone who wants to show up online with one real story a week.

**Before you start**
- Your profile is in the folder: `profile/profile.md`. Add 2 or 3 things you have really written to `profile/voice-samples.md`, so the posts sound like you.

**How to run it**
Open Claude Code in this folder and type: `/post-writer`
It asks about one real thing that happened: the situation, what you did, what you learned.

**What you get**
Three short versions of a post (5 to 8 lines each) in your voice. You pick one and adjust it. Nothing is posted for you. Saved in `outputs/post-writer/`.

**What it will not do**
It will not invent facts about you, promise money or clients, or post anything for you. You stay in charge of every decision.

**Next**
Post it yourself. Come back with another real story any time.
