# Ideal Customer Interviewer

**The problem it solves**
"I know roughly who I help, but I cannot picture one real person, so I do not know what to say to them."

**Who it is for**
Anyone who has chosen a niche and wants to picture their customer.

**Before you start**
- Your profile is in the folder: `profile/profile.md`. Your niche should be filled in. If it is not, run `/niche-finder` first.

**How to run it**
Open Claude Code in this folder and type: `/ideal-customer-interviewer`
It asks about ONE real person you know who fits your niche. Think of a real person, not an average one.

**What you get**
A clear picture of your ideal customer, a friendly script for a 15-minute conversation (a non-sales opening, 10 open questions, 3 questions about what they have tried, a polite close), and one thing to do this week. Saved in `outputs/ideal-customer-interviewer/`.

**What it will not do**
It will not invent facts about you, promise money or clients, or post anything for you. You stay in charge of every decision.

**Next**
Have your real conversation, then run `/offer-builder`.
