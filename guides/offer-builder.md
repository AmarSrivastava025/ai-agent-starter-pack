# Offer Builder

**The problem it solves**
"I know who I help, but I have nothing concrete to put in front of them."

**Who it is for**
Anyone with a niche and a customer in mind.

**Before you start**
- Your profile is in the folder: `profile/profile.md`. Your niche and your ideal customer should be filled in.

**How to run it**
Open Claude Code in this folder and type: `/offer-builder`
It asks what you plan to do with the person, how long it takes, what they bring and what is not included.

**What you get**
A one-page offer: who it is for, the problem in their words, what you do together, how long, what they bring, what is not included, how you start, and your price left as [my price]. Every [square bracket] is a detail only you can fill in. Plus three worries a careful person may have, with calm answers. Saved in `outputs/offer-builder/`.

**What it will not do**
It will not invent facts about you, promise money or clients, or post anything for you. You stay in charge of every decision.
