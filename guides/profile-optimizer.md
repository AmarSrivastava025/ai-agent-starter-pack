# Profile Optimizer

**The problem it solves**
"People visit my profile and still cannot tell what I do or who I help."

**Who it is for**
Anyone with a LinkedIn or Instagram profile that does not say who they help.

**Before you start**
- Your profile is in the folder: `profile/profile.md`. Your niche and customer should be filled in. Have your current profile text ready to paste.

**How to run it**
Open Claude Code in this folder and type: `/profile-optimizer`
It asks which platform, asks you to paste your current text, and asks for the length limit shown on the screen where you edit it.

**What you get**
A five-second test of your current profile, three rewrites of your headline or bio, a rewritten About section in your voice, and a short list of what to change. Nothing is changed on your profile. You copy and paste it yourself. Saved in `outputs/profile-optimizer/`.

**What it will not do**
It will not invent facts about you, promise money or clients, or post anything for you. You stay in charge of every decision.
