## B. Five niches to consider, best first

**1. People who get very nervous before a job interview**
- Who needs it: a person who has an interview coming and is very nervous about it.
- Their problem: "I get very nervous before a job interview."
- Why it fits you: Priya, your calm in tense moments, and your skill at explaining what a company is looking for.
- What could get in the way: with about 5 hours a week, you can only work with a few people at a time. Finding them without cold calling or videos will take thought.
- One small test this week: ask one person you know who has an interview coming if they would like to practise with you for an hour. Do not sell anything.

**2. People who have to hold a hard conversation with a team member**
- Who needs it: a person who must have a difficult talk with someone in their team.
- Their problem: "I do not know how to start this conversation."
- Why it fits you: colleagues already ask you to help them prepare, and you stay calm when talk gets tense.
- What could get in the way: these talks are often about a person inside a company, so privacy matters. Your colleagues may be the only people you can reach at first.
- One small test this week: ask one colleague who recently asked for this help what was hardest for them.

**3. People who have to write a job description and are stuck**
- Who needs it: a person who has to write a job description and does not know what to put in it.
- Their problem: "I do not know how to write this so the right people want it."
- Why it fits you: colleagues already ask you for this, and you know what companies look for.
- What could get in the way: it is more of a document task and less about the person, which may feel different from the work you enjoy most.
- One small test this week: ask one colleague who asked you for this what they found hardest about it.

**4. People who want to know what a company is really looking for in an interview**
- Who needs it: a person who is going to an interview and is not sure what the company wants to hear.
- Their problem: "I do not know what they want from me."
- Why it fits you: you can explain what a company is really looking for.
- What could get in the way: it is close to niche 1, so it may be better as a part of it than on its own.
- One small test this week: ask one person who has been to an interview what they wish they had known.

**5. People in charge of welcoming new joiners**
- Who needs it: a person who looks after new joiners in a team and wants them to feel looked after.
- Their problem: "People leave early and I do not know why."
- Why it fits you: your day 10 check-in, and fewer joiners left early.
- What could get in the way: your result is from inside your own company. You have not yet tried it with other teams, and 5 hours a week is not much for work that goes deep.
- One small test this week: post your day 10 check-in idea and read who answers.
