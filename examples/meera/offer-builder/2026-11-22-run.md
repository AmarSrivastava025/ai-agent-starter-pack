## 1. Who it is for
People who get very nervous before a job interview. The person I have in mind is Priya, 28, an engineer at a mid-size company, who is thinking about changing jobs.

## 2. The problem in their words
[to check after my first conversation]

## 3. What we will do together
1. I listen to what you did in your last job.
2. We practise interview questions together.
3. I help you put your stories into simple words.

## 3 worries a careful person may have about this offer

**1. "If I pay for help, does it mean I am not good enough?"**
No. Getting ready with someone else is a normal way to prepare, and it says nothing about how good you are. The work is about saying what you really did, in your own words.

**2. "I already tried learning answers, and I went blank. Why would this be different?"**
We start with what you really did in your last job, not with answers written for you. I cannot promise how you will feel in the interview, but you will practise saying your own stories out loud with someone, not alone.

**3. "Are two sessions enough?"**
I need to decide this. I will be honest about what two one-hour sessions can and cannot cover, and I will not promise a result.
