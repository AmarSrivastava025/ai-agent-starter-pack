## B. Questions to ask a real person (15-minute conversation)

### Opening (about 30 seconds, not a pitch)
"Hi Priya, thank you for making time. I am learning how people feel about job interviews, and I would love to hear how it has really been for you. There are no right answers. I will not sell you anything. I just want to listen. Is it okay if I ask a few questions and take some notes?"

### 10 questions about things that really happened
1. Tell me about the last interview you went to. What happened, from the moment you woke up?
2. Think of the moment you froze. What was the question? What did you do next?
3. What was going through your head in the minutes before you walked in?
4. How did the days before that interview go? What did you do with your time?
5. What happened right after the interview ended? What did you do, and who did you tell?
6. Tell me about a time at work when you explained what you did, and it went well. What was different about that moment?
7. Think of a project you are proud of. How would you tell it to a friend over tea?
8. When you reply to someone's message about your work, how do you usually write it? (Then compare with how you spoke in the interview.)
9. Who did you talk to about your nerves, if anyone? What did they say?
10. Tell me about the last time you looked for help with something at work or in life. How did you decide whom to ask?

### 3 questions about what she already tried
1. You watched videos and read lists of questions. What did you do with them, step by step?
2. Tell me about the time you memorised an answer. What happened when you said it out loud?
3. Has anyone ever practised with you, in person or on a call? What was that like?

### Polite closing
"Thank you, this really helped me. May I come back to you if I have one more small question? And if there is anything you want to ask me, please do."
