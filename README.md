# AI Agent Starter Pack: 5 free helpers for your second income

You have a job and years of experience. You want to earn from it on the side, but you are stuck on questions like these:

- What could I actually sell?
- Who is the one person I would help?
- What exactly would I offer them?
- My profile does not say who I help.
- I do not know what to post.

This pack has five helpers, one for each question. Each one asks you questions, one at a time, and writes the result in your own words.

| Order | Type this | The problem it solves |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/niche-finder` | "I do not know what to sell." It interviews you and names the niche to test first. |
| 2 | `/ideal-customer-interviewer` | "I cannot picture my customer." It builds one real person and the questions to ask them. |
| 3 | `/offer-builder` | "I have no clear offer." It writes your one-page offer. |
| 4 | `/profile-optimizer` | "My profile does not say who I help." It rewrites your LinkedIn or Instagram text. |
| 5 | `/post-writer` | "I do not know what to post." It turns one real experience into a post. |

## What you need
- A Windows or Mac computer.
- **VS Code** (free).
- **A Claude Pro subscription.** This is the one paid item. Check the current price on claude.ai.

## Set up (about 30 minutes, once)
1. Click the green **Code** button on this page, then **Download ZIP**, and unzip it. (Or run `git clone` if you know how.)
2. Install VS Code from code.visualstudio.com (version 1.94 or newer).
3. In VS Code, install the extension called **Claude Code** (from Anthropic) and sign in with your Claude account.
4. Choose File, then Open Folder, and pick the unzipped folder.
5. Open `START-HERE.md` and follow it. It also shows you how to fill in your short profile.

## Good to know
- Nothing is posted or changed anywhere for you. You copy what you like and use it yourself.
- Your answers stay as files on your own computer.
- These helpers are for learning and practice. They do not promise money, clients or income.
- The `examples` folder shows real results for a made-up person, so you can see what to expect.
